# Postpone your Worry 

This approach involves postponing your worry to a ’worry period’ that is the same time, place, and duration each day. It is important to practice this approach as it will take some time to develop your skill and gain some benefits. It may be best to start by practicing with minor concerns before moving onto major worries.  Read the information sheet **_Postpone your Worry_** before tackling this exercise.
