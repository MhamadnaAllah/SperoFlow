# Letting Go with Mindfulness 

This approach involves learning how to use mindfulness to let go of worrisome or negative thinking. The purpose of this worksheet is to record your practice of using mindfulness when distressing thoughts occur. Use the **Letting Go Scale** to rate how well you are able to ‘let go’ of negative thoughts. You may need to refer to other Mindfulness information sheets.
