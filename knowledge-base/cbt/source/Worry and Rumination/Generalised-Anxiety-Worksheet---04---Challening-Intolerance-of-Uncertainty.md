# Challenging Intolerance of Uncertainty 

Do the exercise below and ask yourself some questions to dissect your intolerance of uncertainty and question your need for certainty. These questions can help you to see that trying to eliminate uncertainty and unpredictability from your life is both impossible and unhelpful, prolonging worry.
