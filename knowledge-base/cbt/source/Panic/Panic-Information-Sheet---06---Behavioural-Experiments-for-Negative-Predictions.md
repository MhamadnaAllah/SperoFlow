For example, let us imagine you have been invited to a BBQ and your negative prediction is: “ _I will have a terrible time, no-one will speak to me, I will feel like a total fool._ ” 

_There might not be anyone I know at the BBQ. But I will at least know the host and I can ask to be introduced to some other people._ 

Your usual response may be to either avoid the BBQ altogether, or to attend but to leave as soon as you feel uncomfortable, or to stand in the corner and speak only to one person you already know. This may help you reduce your discomfort in the short term, but it also contributes to the continuation of your negative predictions, and this means continuation of anxieties.
