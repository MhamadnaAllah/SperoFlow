# **Dietary Guidelines During Recovery** 

1. **Eat by the clock** (i.e. 3 meals, 2-3 snacks, about 3 hours apart). 

2.  Aim to **eat mechanically** while you are practising normal eating. 

3. Plan your meals and snacks in advance. 



4. At lunch and dinner aim to follow the **Thirds Rule** (i.e. 1/3 carb, 1/3 protein, 1/3 vegetables). 

5. Watch the **fillers or “volume eating”** (i.e. _foods that fill you up without offering sufficient nutrition)_ as they keep you focussed on restricting food.  They often make you feel less satisfied too! 

6. Watch the **diet foods** as they keep you connected to your eating disorder and focussed on restricting food. 

7. Don’t forget the **fun foods** and the essential fatty acids. 

8. Keep a check on your **fluid intake** (i.e. _not too much & not too little)._ 



9. Remember the **4E’s RULE** = Exercise Equals Extra Eating. 

10. Try to **eat with support** (with a friend, family member, partner, or carer) for your meal and stay with them for one hour after the meal. 



## _Remind yourself of the_ **_big picture_** _. Stand back and remind yourself what you’re working towards._
